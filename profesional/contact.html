Good to know

How do I book a tour?
Send us a WhatsApp message or call +506 8471 6533 with the tour you like, the date and how many people are coming. We'll answer with the details.
How much do the tours cost?
Call or text us for details and pricing. The price depends on the tour and on how many people are in your group.
Do you offer tours that aren't on the list?
We do all kinds of tours. If there's something you'd like to do in Guanacaste, ask us.
Where can we meet?
